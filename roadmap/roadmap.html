Rules I keep

  • Three goals a day. Never four.
  • Anything of value, a fee or a signature waits for the lawyer.
  • No number without a source.
  • Sleep 7+ hours. Health first.
  • Reserves are never deal money.

How the best in this field work (my judgment, not a study)

They ship something every week, not just study. They write down predictions and score themselves. They spend more time with people than with courses, because in deal work access and judgment beat technical polish. They protect sleep and say no to side projects. They stay patient: one good deal, done well, opens more doors than ten rushed ones.
