01 — The business in one page

Measure. Certify. Then insure.

If you read nothing else, read these three.

We install a flight recorder on a company’s AI, turn the recording into a certificate their customers accept, and then insure it — because we already know how it behaves.

We measure

Paid assessments and continuous telemetry that record what a customer’s AI actually did — every output, every refusal, every drift from tested behavior — against the ISO/IEC 42001 management standard.

A flight recorder for AI.

We certify

That record becomes a defensible artifact: a score a board can sign, a buyer can demand in procurement, and a regulator can inspect.

A certificate their customers and regulators accept.

Then we insure

Only monitored customers can buy the cover. The telemetry is the underwriting file — the one thing every competitor in this market lacks.

We can price what nobody else can price. That is the whole advantage.

Why this order matters

An insurer will not hand a new company the pen without loss data. You cannot get loss data without customers. You cannot get customers for insurance nobody has priced. The measurement business breaks that loop: sellable on day one, needs no regulator and no carrier, and it manufactures exactly the data the insurance leg requires. Our simulation puts the correct sequence at 2.3× the odds of reaching the full stack versus insuring first.

Sell the measuring tape before the insurance. You need the measurements to price the insurance at all.
