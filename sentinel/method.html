01 — Method

Show the working, or do not show the number.

Three models sit behind this site. All three are reproducible from the files shipped alongside it, with fixed seeds.

Everything on this site comes from code you can re-run. Here is what each model does.

Model 01

Actuarial pricing

Standard excess-of-loss ratemaking. Poisson frequency, modified by sector and revenue band; a three-component lognormal severity mixture; aggregate annual loss ceded to layer; then 28% expense, 10% profit and a risk load proportional to the layer’s coefficient of variation.

400,000 simulations · seed 4711 · pricing.py

Model 02

Strategy viability

Three go-to-market strategies over five years. Success is defined strictly: still holds carrier capacity, clears $10M of year-five revenue, and is cumulatively cash-positive. Assurance-first wins at 81.6% against 2.4% and 0.2%.

60,000 trials · seed 20260808 · montecarlo_v2.py

Model 03

Evidence grading

Twenty-nine claims, each graded A to D by source quality. Four were discarded and are shown struck through rather than deleted, so a reader can see what was rejected and why.

evidence.json · read the ledger

Severity calibration

A severity model is only worth something if it reproduces outcomes that actually happened. Ours is tuned until its quantiles land on the five adjudicated results:

Simulated severity quantiles against real adjudicated outcomes
Quantile Model Real-world anchor

We kept adjusting the model until the disasters it predicts match the disasters that actually happened.

What we refuse to do

  • ×Quote a layer whose rate on line exceeds 4%. At that burn rate the policy is a payment plan, not a risk transfer.
  • ×Present a competitor’s rate as fact. Lloyd’s and surplus-lines rates are not publicly filed; ours are labeled simulated.
  • ×Use a market figure we cannot trace to a primary source, however widely it is repeated.

If we cannot show you where a number came from, it does not go on the site.
