# Deal Monte Carlo

5,000 draws of ARV and rehab overrun; a deal "works" if the buyer's margin clears the minimum and it closes. The example deal works in about 29% of outcomes (Excel 29.5%, Python 29.3%). A solver finds the highest contract price that works 60% of the time. Part of [[Deal Analyzer v2]].
