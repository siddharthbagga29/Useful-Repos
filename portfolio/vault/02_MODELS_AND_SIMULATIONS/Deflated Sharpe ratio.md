# Deflated Sharpe ratio

The probability that a strategy's true Sharpe ratio beats what the best of N unskilled strategies would show by luck, adjusting for skew, fat tails and sample length. N is every strategy the [[Brain evolution loop]] has ever tried, so the bar rises as the search runs. Source: [[Bailey & López de Prado 2014]].
