# Defense over offense

**Claim.** Simple defensive rules do not reliably beat an equity index on return. What they reliably do is lose less in bad markets.

**Evidence.** Across 1,000 ten-year [[Block bootstrap Monte Carlo]] paths, no rule beats SPY's return reliably, but the 60/40 mix has a shallower worst drawdown than SPY in 99.8% of paths, inverse volatility in 96% and volatility targeting in 92%. See [[Strategy Lab rules]].

**Why it matters.** For a family that thinks in decades, avoiding the drawdown that forces a sale matters more than the last point of return.
