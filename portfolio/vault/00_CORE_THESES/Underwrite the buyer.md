# Underwrite the buyer

**Claim.** A wholesale assignment only closes if the end buyer still makes money after their own selling, closing and holding costs. A deal that clears the 70% rule can still be a NO-GO.

**Evidence.** The example deal in [[Deal Analyzer v2]] clears v1's checks but leaves the buyer a 17.5% margin against a 20% minimum once buyer costs are counted. The [[Deal Monte Carlo]] says it works in about 29% of outcomes. Found in [[Deal Analyzer v1 audit]].
