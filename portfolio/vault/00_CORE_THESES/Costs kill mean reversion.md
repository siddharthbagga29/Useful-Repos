# Costs kill mean reversion

**Claim.** Online portfolio selection's headline winners (PAMR, OLMAR) earn their edge by trading most of the book every week. Once you pay for that trading, the edge is gone.

**Evidence.** In [[OPS replication]], PAMR compounds at 10.5% a year with zero costs but turns over about 55% of the portfolio weekly. The [[Cost sweep]] puts its break-even at about 5 bps per side. Out of sample at 10 bps it earns 0.8% a year, against 12.1% for holding SPY.

**Status.** Supported. Prior work: [[Li & Hoi survey]], [[Lahanis, Liu & Zhou]].
