Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026 · Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India)

This policy explains what personal data High Properties collects through www.highproperties.in, why we collect it, and the choices you have. High Properties is the Data Fiduciary for this data.

In short: we collect the details you type into our enquiry form, we use them only to respond to your property enquiry, we never sell them, and you can ask us to delete them at any time by emailing highproperties9@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

High Properties, proprietor Yoogesh Bagga, operating in Gurugram and Delhi NCR, Haryana, India.
Contact: +91 98215 53693 · highproperties9@gmail.com

2. What we collect

  • Details you give us — name, mobile number, email address, the property type, budget and location you are interested in, and anything you write in the message box or send us on WhatsApp.
  • Technical data — IP address and basic request information, kept briefly to prevent spam and abuse of our forms.
  • Analytics — only if you accept the cookie banner. Decline and no analytics or advertising scripts are loaded at all.

3. Why we use it

  • To call, email or message you about the enquiry you sent us.
  • To shortlist properties matching your requirement.
  • To meet our record-keeping obligations as a property broker.
  • To keep our website secure and working.

We rely on the consent you give when you tick the consent box on our form. You can withdraw it at any time.

4. WhatsApp

If you contact us on WhatsApp, or tick the box allowing us to reach you there, your message and number are processed through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. Their handling of that data is governed by Meta's own privacy terms. We use it only to reply to you about property matters.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only where necessary with:

  • our web hosting provider, which stores the site and its database;
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), where you have chosen to talk to us there;
  • a builder, seller, buyer or lender only where you have asked us to introduce you;
  • professional advisers or authorities where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for up to three years from your last contact with us, after which they are deleted. Anti-spam technical logs are kept for a few days. If you ask us to erase your data sooner, we will, unless a law requires us to retain it.

7. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct or complete anything inaccurate.
  • Ask us to erase your data.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time — this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unsatisfied.

To exercise any of these, email highproperties9@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDP request". We aim to respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site and any staff sign-in working. Analytics and advertising cookies load only after you press Accept on the cookie banner. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Staff passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and staff areas are protected by server-side sessions. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 18.
