# PART XVI — Business Preparation

## §1. The line this file will not cross

⚠️ **Everything here is preparation. None of it is execution.**

You have told me you are not currently authorised for employment. Whether a
given activity constitutes "employment" or "unauthorized work" — including
unpaid work, self-employment, forming an entity, or accepting anything of value —
is a legal question I am not able to answer for you.

**Anything marked 🔒 below requires an attorney's clearance before you do it.**
Do not treat my categorisation as permission; treat it as a list of things to
ask about. See `18-immigration-counsel-questions.md`.

| Generally understood as preparation | Requires counsel before acting 🔒 |
|---|---|
| Market research and reading | Forming an LLC or any entity |
| Writing a business plan for future use | Registering a business name or obtaining an EIN |
| Building financial models for your own plan | Opening a business bank account |
| Learning skills | Signing any client agreement, paid or unpaid |
| Talking to potential future customers about their problems | Accepting money, equity, or anything of value |
| Networking | Doing "trial", "sample" or "pilot" work for a company |
| Preparing marketing materials you do not publish | Publishing anything that solicits business |
| Studying competitors | Anything with a fee schedule attached |

**[I]** The recurring trap for people in your position is the "unpaid trial
project" — a firm you have been networking with says *"why don't you take a look
at this deal and tell us what you think?"* It feels like networking. It may not
be. Ask counsel about that exact scenario **before** it is offered, because it
will be offered, and you will want an answer in the moment rather than a week
later.

## §2. Business options, ranked by fit with your actual evidence

### Option 1 — Acquisition entrepreneurship (self-funded search or LDE)
**Fit: high, long-dated.**
[V, A10] You have underwritten acquisitions and written LP materials at 23. This
is the natural terminal destination of everything else in this system.

**Reality check.** [R] Stanford's 2026 study tracks 862 search funds; ~58% have
historically acquired a company, but only **48% of the 2021–2024 cohorts** had
acquired. Classic search requires investor backing that generally follows an MBA
network or an operating track record. You are 23, with about a year of
professional experience and no capital. **[I] This is a 5–10 year path, not a
six-month one, and anyone telling you otherwise is selling something.**

**What to do now:** build the criteria doc (Project 6), build the proprietary
sourcing list, know the searcher community, and — critically — **spend three to
five years earning and saving in your natural market first.** The search fund
route is dramatically easier with $150k of savings and a deal résumé than with
neither.

### Option 2 — Independent-sponsor deal participation
**Fit: high, medium-dated.**
[I] More realistic than a search fund because it does not require you to raise a
search vehicle. You join or support a sponsor, contribute analytical work, and
over time participate in a deal. The route runs through employment first, which
`14-ead-arrival-and-100-day.md` handles.

### Option 3 — Financial modelling / valuation services for SMBs
**Fit: high skill match. Serious cautions.** 🔒
[V, A10] You can do this work today. Three problems: (a) it is unambiguously
work, and 🔒; (b) valuation for third-party reliance can touch professional
licensing and standards regimes depending on the purpose (tax, litigation, ESOP,
transaction opinions) — ask a CPA and an attorney which uses require what
credential; (c) it competes for hours with the portfolio and the network, which
have higher long-run value for you.

**[REC]** Treat this as a possible bridge *after* authorisation if the job search
runs long — not as a plan. Do not build a client base while unauthorised.

### Option 4 — Deal sourcing / origination support for sponsors
**Fit: high. Compensation structure is the problem.** 🔒
Sourcing fees or success fees for introducing deals can implicate broker-dealer
registration under securities law. This is a genuinely regulated area, and it is
also 🔒 for immigration reasons. **[REC]** Understand it; do not act on it
without both securities and immigration counsel.

### Option 5 — Investment research / newsletter
**Fit: medium.** 🔒 the moment it is monetised.
[I] Unmonetised writing published on your own initiative is closer to the
"portfolio" activity already in this system, and `07-investment-portfolio.md` §5
covers it. Publishing your sector research for reputation is the version to do.
Charging for it is a different activity entirely.

### Option 6 — Something in the family/small-business services space
**Fit: unknown.** [I] Your Turnkey experience [V, A10] gave you operating
exposure to a real services business — vendor negotiation, subcontractor costs,
job costing, payroll. That is genuine SMB operating knowledge that most finance
people lack. Worth thinking about as a future *acquisition target profile* rather
than as a business to start from nothing.

## §3. The blueprint to write now (and file)

One document, 8–12 pages, in `/05 Financial` or a `/Business` folder. Written for
your own future use, not published, not shown to prospective customers.

1. **Thesis** — what you would buy or build, and why you specifically
2. **Market** — from your Project 1 research; you will already have this
3. **Target profile** — size, sector, ownership, geography, owner situation
4. **Sourcing plan** — brokers, proprietary outreach, your CRM
5. **Capital plan** — equity required, debt structure, SBA 7(a) parameters,
   sponsor/investor options
6. **Personal capital plan** — savings needed and by when (`16` §7)
7. **Operating plan** — first 100 days post-close
8. **Return model** — base/upside/downside, with the returns attribution you
   built for Project 2
9. **Risk register** — including the honest one: your age and thin operating track
   record are the binding constraint on raising outside capital
10. **Activation gates** — what must be legally true, financially true, and
    professionally true before any step begins

**[I]** Item 10 is the one that makes this document safe to write and genuinely
useful later. Each gate is a testable condition, not an aspiration.

## §4. Realistic sequencing

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Prepare** | Now – authorisation | Blueprint, criteria, sourcing list, network. No entity, no clients, no money. |
| **Earn** | Years 1–3 post-auth | Role in your natural market. Deal reps, real references, savings. This phase is not a detour — it is the qualification. |
| **Position** | Years 3–5 | Deal participation with a sponsor; co-invest if offered; visible track record |
| **Execute** | Years 5–10 | Search, independent sponsorship, or acquisition with backing |

**[I] The thing worth internalising.** The gap between "can underwrite a deal"
and "can be trusted with capital" is not analytical — you are already close on
the analytical side. It is **track record and time**. The six-month system builds
the relationships that make years 1–3 possible; years 1–3 build the evidence that
makes years 5–10 possible. There is no version of this that skips the middle, and
the people who try are the ones who end up back at the start with less money.
