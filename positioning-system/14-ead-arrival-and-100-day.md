# PART XIV & XV — Activation Day and the 100-Day Launch

## §1. The principle

**No preparation after authorisation. Only execution.**

Everything in this file must be *finished and sitting in a folder* before the
email arrives. On the day itself you should be sending, not writing. The single
test: if you got the notification at 9am on a Tuesday, could you have sent
fifteen messages by noon without opening a blank document?

⚠️ Confirm with counsel what document, and what date on it, actually establishes
your authorisation to begin work, and what your employer will need for I-9
purposes. **Do not infer this from the roadmap.** See
`18-immigration-counsel-questions.md` Q9–Q12.

## §3. Activation timeline

### Within 24 hours
```
☐ Update LinkedIn: remove the pending-status line; add "authorized to work,
  no sponsorship required" where it fits naturally
☐ Message all A-tier contacts (15-20), individually written, using their history
☐ Message the 5 people who answered the month-5 "who should I talk to?" question
☐ Submit the 5 highest-probability prepared applications
☐ Email membership orgs: your status has changed, you are looking
☐ Tell the Turnkey Managing Partner and your Cerity contacts first — warmest,
  and they may move fastest
```

The A-tier message:
> [Name] — quick update: my work authorisation came through this week, so I'm
> now able to take a role. No sponsorship needed, now or ever.
>
> You'll remember I've been working through the [sector] thesis — [one line on
> where it landed]. I'm targeting [specific seat] in the Cincinnati LMM market.
>
> If anyone comes to mind, I'd be grateful. And either way, thank you for the
> conversations over the last months — they shaped what I went after.

**[I]** It works because it is a *continuation*. This is precisely what the
month-6 reconnect sweep buys: nobody is hearing from a stranger.

### Within 72 hours
```
☐ All 30 prepared applications submitted
☐ Every B-tier contact messaged (30-40)
☐ 3 recruiters contacted, portfolio attached
☐ Alumni network post
☐ Calendar reset: interviews take priority over everything in this repo
```

### Within 7 days
```
☐ Follow up on every application that has a named human attached
☐ Book coffee with 5 A-tier contacts — in person, this week
☐ Attend the next event of each Tier 1 org, with the new status
☐ First interviews scheduled
☐ Post the portfolio publicly with the status update
```

### Within 30 days
```
☐ 40+ conversations
☐ 10+ first-round interviews
☐ Every A-tier contact spoken to, not just messaged
☐ Second-round preparation using project artifacts as the discussion material
☐ Reassess: which of the 14 paths is actually responding? Reallocate.
```

## §4. The 100-day launch

Quality over volume, deliberately.

| Metric | Target | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Substantive conversations | 100 | ~1/day; most from the existing network |
| Applications submitted | 40 | 30 prepared + 10 emergent |
| **Applications with a referral** | **20** | The number that matters — see below |
| First-round interviews | 15 | |
| Final rounds | 5 | |
| Offers | 2 | Two, so you can choose |
| Events attended | 12 | Do not stop attending once interviewing |
| Portfolio presentations | 10 | Walking someone through a memo *is* the interview |
| Target employers engaged | 30 | |
| Business exploration | Ongoing | `15-business-preparation.md`, now legally executable |

**[R]** Burks et al. (*QJE* 2015): referred workers are 10–30% less likely to
quit, perform better on high-impact metrics, and are more profitable per worker —
with the effect concentrated in referrals from high-quality employees.
**[I] Implication:** twenty referred applications is a materially different
process from forty cold ones, and it is the entire reason the preceding six
months were spent on relationships rather than on applications. Do not let the
comfort of the apply button pull you back to volume.

### The 100-day rhythm
- **Weeks 1–2** — activation blitz; the system's daily loop pauses; conversations
  and applications are the whole job
- **Weeks 3–6** — interview cycles; keep 3 networking conversations a week going
  regardless, because pipelines die when they feel unnecessary
- **Weeks 7–10** — second rounds and case work; use portfolio artifacts as the
  substrate
- **Weeks 11–14** — negotiation; **restart the daily loop** the day you accept

**[I]** Restarting the loop on acceptance day is the difference between a job and
a career. The network you built to get hired is the network that sources your
first deal, your first co-invest, and eventually your own fund. The CRM does not
retire.
