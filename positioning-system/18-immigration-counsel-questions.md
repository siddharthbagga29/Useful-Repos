# Immigration — Questions for Your Attorney

## §0. What this file is and is not

**I am not a lawyer and this is not legal advice.** Nothing here tells you what
is permissible. It is a list of questions to take to a licensed U.S. immigration
attorney, written so that one consultation answers as much as possible.

**[REC] Book this consultation in week 1.** It is the highest-priority action in
the entire system after fixing the résumé, because several other decisions in
this repo are gated on the answers, and because two of the questions below
concern things that may already have happened.

Where to find counsel: AILA's referral directory (<https://ailalawyer.com>) or
the Cincinnati Bar Association referral service. **[I]** Ask for someone whose
practice includes family-based adjustment of status *and* employment
authorisation questions — not a firm that only does employment-based petitions.

Bring: your I-485 receipt notice, I-765 receipt notice if filed, I-94 history,
your most recent OPT EAD, your I-20s, your marriage certificate, your spouse's
proof of citizenship, and — importantly — **a written timeline of the Turnkey
Services Pro employment (Aug 2025 – Jun 2026) and the Cerity Partners internship
(Jan – Mar 2025)**, with the authorisation you were relying on for each.

## §1. Status and the record so far

**Q1.** What is my current status and what, precisely, am I authorised to do
today? Please put it in writing.

**Q2.** My résumé currently states *"Authorized to work in the U.S. with no
sponsorship required (pending adjustment of status, immediate relative of U.S.
citizen)."* Given my actual status, is that statement accurate? If not, what
should it say, and does its prior circulation create any issue I need to address?

**Q3.** I worked at Turnkey Services Pro from August 2025 to June 2026, across a
promotion. Was that employment within my authorisation for its entire duration?
If any part of it was not, what are the consequences for my pending I-485, and
what should I do now?

**Q4.** Does anything in my history — the OPT period, the grace period, any gap —
create an unauthorised-employment or status issue that could affect my
adjustment of status? Is there an INA §245(k) or similar consideration I should
understand?

## §2. What I may do while waiting

For each of the following, please tell me whether it constitutes employment or
unauthorised work in my current status:

**Q5.** Publishing independent investment research and sector analysis on my own
initiative, with no compensation of any kind, on my own website or LinkedIn.

**Q6.** Performing an unpaid "sample", "trial" or "test" analysis **at the
request of a company** — for example, a private equity firm asking me to look at
a deal and share my view. Does the request change the analysis versus work I
initiate myself?

**Q7.** Volunteering for a professional organisation (ACG, CFA Society) in a role
that is customarily unpaid, such as event support or a committee.

**Q8.** Attending conferences, informational interviews, and networking events;
and separately, whether any of these become problematic if a firm reimburses my
travel or waives a fee.

**Q9.** Preparing job applications and interview materials now for submission
after authorisation; and whether I may **interview** with employers before I am
authorised, including whether I may accept an offer contingent on authorisation.

**Q10.** Forming an LLC, obtaining an EIN, opening a business bank account, or
registering a business name — with no revenue and no operations.

**Q11.** Accepting any of the following: money, equity, deferred compensation, a
finder's fee, a gift, or reimbursement, in connection with any analysis I produce.

**Q12.** Continuing an unpaid working relationship with my former employer
(Turnkey Services Pro) in any capacity — advisory, informal, or otherwise.

## §3. Timing, documents and travel

**Q13.** What is the realistic current processing time for my I-765 (c)(9) and my
I-485, given where they were filed? What can cause delay, and what should I be
monitoring?

**Q14.** Which document — and which date on it — establishes that I may begin
work? What exactly will an employer need for I-9 purposes on my first day?

**Q15.** I understand DHS published an interim final rule (90 FR 48799, effective
2025-10-30) removing automatic EAD extensions for renewals filed on or after that
date, and that (c)(9) is not among the exceptions. How does that affect my
renewal timing, and when should I file to avoid a gap?

**Q16.** May I travel domestically? May I travel internationally, and do I need
advance parole? Would departure affect my pending applications? (I may want to
attend conferences in Dallas in October and New York in September.)

**Q17.** Do I need to report a change of address, employment, or marital
circumstances to USCIS during this period? What are the deadlines?

**Q18.** Is there anything about my case that would make you advise against a
particular category of activity that I have not asked about?

## §5. The standing rule

**When in doubt, it is 🔒.**

If an activity involves *doing something of value for another party*, treat it as
requiring clearance regardless of whether money changes hands, regardless of how
it is labelled, and regardless of how enthusiastic the other party is. The cost
of one extra email to your attorney is trivial. The cost of a mistake on a
pending adjustment of status is not.
