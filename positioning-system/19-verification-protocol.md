# Verification Protocol

## §1. Source hierarchy

| Tier | Sources | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Primary** | SEC EDGAR · USCIS/DHS/Federal Register · BLS · Census · Ohio SOS · SEC IAPD (Form ADV) · FINRA BrokerCheck · a firm's own website · a university's own site · peer-reviewed journals | Assert freely |
| **2 — Institutional** | Federal Reserve and district banks · major consultancy and accounting research · established financial press with named authors · Stanford GSB and comparable institutional studies | Assert with attribution |
| **3 — Trade** | Trade associations · industry databases (PitchBook, Axial, PrivSource) · local business journals | Attribute and, if it matters, corroborate |
| **4 — Social** | LinkedIn · Reddit · forums · marketing blogs · aggregator "company profile" sites · SEO listicles | **Discovery only. Never a cited fact.** |

**[I]** A large share of what a search engine returns for "top PE firms in
[city]" is Tier 4 content written to rank, not to inform. It is genuinely useful
for finding out that a firm *exists*. It is not usable for anything you would say
in a room.

---

## §2. Verification states

| State | Means | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Confirmed from a Tier 1 primary source, with URL and date | Free to use in writing and conversation |
| **SECONDARY VERIFIED** | Confirmed from Tier 2, or from two independent Tier 3 sources | Usable **with attribution** — "according to X" |
| **UNVERIFIED** | Not confirmed | **Never used in conversation.** May sit in the database as a lead. |
| **OUTDATED** | Was verified, but more than 90 days ago | Re-verify or delete. Do not use. |

The daily engine downgrades VERIFIED → OUTDATED automatically after 90 days.
**It never upgrades anything.** Promotion to VERIFIED is a human act, always.

---

## §3. What must be verified before you say it out loud

- Any person's **name, title, or firm**
- Any **firm's** AUM, fund size, strategy, or deal
- Any **company's** revenue, ownership, or size
- Any **statistic**
- Any **event** date, cost, or eligibility
- Any **organisation's** membership cost or category
- Any **rule** — credit, regulatory, immigration

**[I] The private-markets-specific reason this matters more for you than for most
people.** In a market of a few hundred professionals, the person you are talking
to very likely knows the firm, the deal, or the individual you just mentioned. A
wrong figure is not corrected politely and forgotten — it is filed. And the job
you are interviewing for is, at bottom, *the job of being right about numbers*.
There is no worse place to be casually wrong.

---

## §4. Where LinkedIn fails specifically

**Fine for:** discovering people exist · seeing what they post · finding shared
connections · confirming a firm has a presence.

**Never sufficient alone for:** titles · tenure · AUM · fund size · deal history ·
education · current employment.

Because: profiles are self-authored, often stale by months or years, sometimes
aspirational, and never audited.

**Verify instead at:** the firm's own team page (Tier 1) · Form ADV via IAPD for
registered advisers (Tier 1, and authoritative for AUM and personnel) ·
BrokerCheck for broker-dealers (Tier 1) · Ohio SOS for entity facts (Tier 1).

---

## §5. The two-minute rule

**Before any fact enters a conversation, you must be able to produce its URL in
under two minutes.**

If you cannot, do not say it. Say instead: *"I think I read that somewhere but I
haven't confirmed it — let me check and come back to you."*

**[I]** That sentence is not a weakness. Said to a senior investor, it is a
credential: it tells them you distinguish between what you know and what you have
heard, which is precisely the discipline they are hiring for and precisely the
discipline most candidates lack.

---

## §6. Verification in this repository

Everything in these files is tagged **[V]** verified in your documents ·
**[R]** external research with a citation · **[I]** my inference ·
**[A]** assumption · **[REC]** recommendation.

Specifically:
- Facts about **you** are [V] from the résumé, transcript, diploma, certificate
  and capstone, or explicitly marked as résumé-only.
- **Firms and organisations** are real but **SECONDARY VERIFIED at best** — most
  came from search results, several from Tier 3–4 pages. Hour 2 of
  `00-START-HERE.md` is you promoting the Tier 1 ones to VERIFIED yourself.
- **Research findings** in `17-evidence-base.md` are Tier 1 with DOIs and URLs.
- **No individual is named** anywhere in this repository. That is deliberate.
- **No private-company financial figure** appears anywhere. Also deliberate.
