- Paid "executive network" memberships that publish no member list.

- LinkedIn premium as a substitute for showing up in a room. It is not.
- Any conference whose attendee list you cannot see before paying.

## The membership-inquiry email

Use in hour 2. Two things it does: asks a real question, and discloses status
without apology.

> **Subject:** Membership category question — recent UC Lindner MS Finance grad
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm a recent MS Finance graduate from UC Lindner, based in Cincinnati, focused
> on lower-middle-market valuation and acquisition underwriting. I spent the
> past year building the finance function and underwriting the acquisition
> pipeline at a small Cincinnati company.
>
> I'm between roles while I complete a green-card process, and I'd like to be
> genuinely involved with [org] during that window rather than after it. Could
> you tell me which membership category fits someone in that position, and
> whether there's a candidate, affiliate or transitional rate?
>
> I'd also like to attend [specific upcoming event] — is that open to
> non-members?
>
> Thanks,
> Siddharth Bagga
> [phone] · [LinkedIn]

**[I]** Naming a specific event is what makes this a real inquiry rather than a
brochure request, and it usually gets you invited to that event whether or not
you join.
