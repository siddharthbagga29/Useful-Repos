Assumes a start on 2026-09-08 and roughly 2.5 hours a day, five days a week
(~50 hrs/month). If your authorisation arrives early, jump straight to
`14-ead-arrival-and-100-day.md` — every month below is designed to be
abandonable at any point without wasting what came before.

## If the timeline moves

**Authorisation arrives early (month 2–3):** stop the roadmap immediately and
activate. An incomplete portfolio plus a live job search beats a complete
portfolio and a late start. Finish P1 only if it is within two weeks of done —
it is the one piece that opens conversations by itself.

**Authorisation takes longer than six months:** do not extend by adding projects.
Extend by **depth on relationships** — the second and third conversations with
existing A-tier contacts are worth more than a seventh portfolio project, and
by month 7 the marginal value of another memo is close to zero while the marginal
value of a partner who has read your work twice is not.
