## Columbus / Indianapolis — the day-trip strategy

**[R]** Columbus: Stonehenge Partners (LMM, mezzanine + equity, founded 1999),
Crane Group (family-owned permanent-capital holding company, founded 1947),
Talisman Capital Partners. ACG Columbus is an active chapter.
**[R]** Indianapolis: Centerfield Equity (LMM business services / healthcare).

**[REC]** One day trip per month, alternating cities, from month 2. Rule: never
travel for a single meeting. A trip is justified at three meetings or one event
plus two meetings. Stack them, leave at 7am, home by 8pm, no hotel cost.

**[I]** Marginal return per contact is *higher* in Columbus and Indianapolis than
Cincinnati, because far fewer Cincinnati-based candidates bother to show up
there. You will be memorable for the fact of the drive alone.

travel situation. **IMMIGRATION COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED** before any travel:
ask specifically about domestic travel and about international travel/advance
parole while an I-485 is pending. Do not book anything before that conversation.
