# Best Direct-Deposit Bank Bonuses: Top 5 Analysis (as of September 22, 2026)

> Offers change often and some are limited by state or by who the bank targets. Always
> read the bank's own offer terms before you open an account. Bonuses count as interest
> income, so the bank reports them on a 1099-INT and you pay tax on them.

## Scoring method

Each offer gets a score out of 10 in four areas. Weights are in parentheses:

| Area | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **Cash bonus (40%)** | Dollar bonus, less any fees you can't reasonably avoid |
| **Ease (25%)** | Direct deposit size and time window, fee waivers, early-closure penalties |
| **Interest / yield (15%)** | What your deposited money earns while it sits there |
| **Leverage (20%)** | Ongoing value: credit-card ecosystem, relationship perks, branch/ATM reach, stackable bonuses |

## 1. Chase Total Checking: $400 (up to $900 combined). Score 8.7/10

**The offer**
- $400 for opening Chase Total Checking and receiving **$1,000+ in direct deposits within 90 days**.
- Chase Savings: **$200** for $15,000 in new money within 30 days, held for 90 days.
- Targeted combo: open both and Chase adds an **extra $400**, for **$900** total ($300 checking + $200 savings + $400).
- Expires **October 14, 2026**.

**Costs and fine print**
- $15/month fee, waived with **$500+ in monthly direct deposits**. A paycheck waives it, so the effective cost is $0.
- Chase can take the bonus back if you close the account within about 6 months.
- Zelle payments and your own transfers **do not** count as direct deposit.

**Interest**: Poor. Chase Savings pays close to 0%. If you park $15,000 there for the $900 combo, you give up roughly $115 to $150 of interest you'd earn at a 3 to 4% high-yield savings account over 90 days. The combo still nets about **$750** more than the checking-only offer.

**Leverage (high)**: The largest branch/ATM network in the U.S. A checking relationship helps when you apply for Chase cards (Sapphire, Freedom, Ink), and the Ultimate Rewards points ecosystem is one of the most valuable. Chase regularly re-offers bonuses (typically every 2 years per product).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9.5 | 9.5 | 4 | 9.5 | **8.7** |

## 2. SoFi Checking & Savings: $400. Score 8.6/10

**The offer**
- **$400** for **$5,000+ in eligible direct deposits within 25 days** of your first direct deposit ($50 tier at $1,000).
- Open to new members and to existing members who never enrolled in direct deposit. Runs through **December 31, 2026**.

**Costs**: No monthly fees, no minimum balance, no overdraft fees.

**Interest (best of the five)**
- **3.10% APY** on savings with direct deposit, plus a **0.90% boost for 6 months**, for **4.00% APY**.
- Example: $10,000 kept at SoFi for 12 months earns about **$355** (6 months at 4.00% plus 6 months at 3.10%), on top of the $400 bonus.

**Leverage (medium-high)**: Early paycheck (up to 2 days), savings "vaults," a single app that also covers SoFi Invest, loans, and credit cards. Member rate discounts on SoFi personal and student loans.

**Watch out**: $5,000 in 25 days means about two biweekly paychecks of $2,500+. With a smaller paycheck, you only get the $50 tier.

- Eligible accounts: Smart Advantage, Smart Money, or Relationship Checking. Open between **September 9 and December 15, 2026**.

**Costs**: Smart Advantage is **$0/month** with paperless statements ($2 otherwise). **$50 fee if you close within 90 days.**

**Interest**: Low on checking. BMO savings rates are moderate.

**Leverage (medium)**: This is the **highest cash bonus** of the five, and the bar is only $1,000/month. The catch is a **7-month commitment**. If you miss one month, the streak ends, because the months must be consecutive.

## Getting the most money: a stacking plan

Most employers let you **split direct deposit** across several accounts. Direct deposits can also be moved every 1 to 2 months. A realistic sequence for someone paid about $2,500 every two weeks:

| Order | Offer | What to do | Cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (now) | **Huntington** (if eligible) | Send one $500 paycheck before 9/30 | $400 |
| 2 | **Chase** | Split $1,000 of your paycheck to Chase | $400 ($900 with combo) |
| 3 | **SoFi** | Send full paychecks for 25 days ($5,000) | $400 |
| 4 | **U.S. Bank** | Route $8,000 over 90 days | $450 |
| 5 | **BMO** (if eligible) | Keep $1,000/month split to BMO for 6 months | $600 |
| | | **Potential total** | **$2,250 to $2,750** |

After the bonuses post, keep your **long-term direct deposit at SoFi** (4.00%, then 3.10% APY, no fees). Keep the Chase or U.S. Bank checking open with the minimum needed to waive fees, and use those relationships for their credit cards.

### Things that can cost you the bonus
1. **Wrong deposit type.** Most banks only count ACH payroll or government benefits (Citi is the exception).
2. **Closing early.** BMO charges $50 within 90 days, and Chase may take back the bonus within about 6 months.
3. **Prior-customer rules.** Most banks exclude anyone who had an account or bonus in the last 12 to 24 months.
4. **ChexSystems.** Opening many accounts quickly can trigger denials. Space out applications.
5. **Taxes.** At a 22% tax bracket, $2,250 in bonuses leaves you about $1,755 after tax.
