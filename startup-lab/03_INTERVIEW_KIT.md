# 03 — Customer interview kit (use only when counsel says conversations are fine; they are learning, not selling)

## Who to talk to (15 needed)
- 5 first-time or aspiring acquirers (searchers, independent-sponsor hopefuls, people who bought a small business)
- 5 small-business brokers or M&A advisors
- 5 owners who sold or are thinking of selling

Find them via ACG/CFA events, searchfunder-style communities, LinkedIn, referrals. Log each in your CRM with source URL.

## Message (≤100 words)
"Hi [name] — I'm researching how first-time buyers evaluate small companies. I built a free checklist from 20 public deals and want to learn what's missing in real life. Could I ask 3 questions in 15 minutes? Not selling anything."

## Questions (past behaviour only)
1. Tell me about the last time you evaluated a small business to buy/sell. What did you do first?
2. What took longest or cost the most? What did you pay for (time or money)?
3. What tools/templates did you use? What was missing?
4. What nearly made you walk away?
5. If a fix existed, who would approve paying for it, and what would it have to be worth?

## After each call (5 min)
Quote 1 sentence verbatim, the cost they named, and whether they asked "can I see it?" (a strong signal).

## Scoring the 15
Pain confirmed (Y/N) · Already paid for a fix (Y/N) · Asked to see it (Y/N). Pass needs 5+ with all three.
