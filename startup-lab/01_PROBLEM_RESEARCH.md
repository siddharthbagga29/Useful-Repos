## What the market already has
Fire inspection software is crowded: Inspect Point, ServiceTrade, Ember, Fire Inspect Hub, others; listed per-user prices run roughly $25–$119/month and conflict across listing sites ([Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/148287/Inspect-Point/), [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/product/533202-Essential)). **A new inspection app has no obvious edge → rejected.**
